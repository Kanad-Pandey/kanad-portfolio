## Solution

Designed and deployed a comprehensive modernization layer on top of the existing CRM:

- **Unified Interface:** Built a custom Power App for simplified field entry.
- **Automated Workflows:** Used Power Automate to handle multi-step approval and notification cycles.
- **Data Centralization:** Leveraged Dataverse for secure, structured storage and integration.

## Impact

- **Transparency:** Increased process visibility for both field teams and management.
- **Adoption:** Improved CRM user adoption through reduced friction.
- **Speed:** Automated notification workflows significantly reduced issue resolution lead times.

## Tech Stack

- **Platform:** Power Apps, Power Automate, Dataverse
- **Integration:** SharePoint, Outlook, Office 365
