## Solution

Designed and implemented a modular launch excellence suite:

- **Market Assessment:** Quantitative opportunity mapping and segmentation of the HCP landscape.
- **Resource Allocation:** Data-driven models for optimal field force sizing and territory alignment.
- **KPI Monitoring:** Real-time dashboards to track launch readiness and early market uptake.

## Impact

- **Readiness:** Provided leadership with clear, data-driven visibility into launch milestones.
- **Decisions:** Supported executive decision-making with evidence-based resource modeling.
- **Governance:** Established a structured, repeatable framework for future launches.
