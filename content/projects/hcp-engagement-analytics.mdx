## Solution

Engineered a unified engagement intelligence platform:

- **Data Fusion:** Built automated pipelines to ingest and normalize data from over 5 disparate sources into a centralized Snowflake repository.
- **Scoring Engine:** Developed a multi-touch attribution model to weight the impact of each interaction on the final prescribing decision.
- **Field Alerts:** Integrated the engine with the field CRM to provide sales reps with real-time "Next Best Action" recommendations.
