## Solution

Leveraged low-code orchestration combined with high-performance LLMs:

- **Orchestration:** Used `n8n` to manage data flow between Google Sheets and the OpenAI API.
- **Prompt Engineering:** Developed sophisticated system prompts to ensure consistent and objective resume scoring.
- **Workflow:** Automated the extraction of resume text, scoring against job descriptions, and returning detailed feedback.
